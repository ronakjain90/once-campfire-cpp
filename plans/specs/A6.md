- `POST /unfurl_link`: the opengraph fetch with its own outbound policy: DNS through the private network guard, the pinned IP, every redirect checked, the 5 MB and 10-redirect caps, the timeouts and the 16-concurrent limit of the Rust README, the 256-attribute `meta` limit. Use blocking I/O on a job thread through `ctx.offload`.
- Active Storage endpoints: blob redirect and proxy, representation (variant and preview) redirect and proxy, direct uploads (authenticated), the disk service GET (with ranges and Content-Disposition) and PUT (checksums), the signed URL checks, the variant and preview creation on first request through the media pool.

- The A3 and A4 sweep requests that wait for this area: the blob redirect (A3) and `/webmanifest` (A4).
